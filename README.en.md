# Metronome

A visual pixel-art metronome with time signatures, accents, subdivisions and tap tempo.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Speed-up programs and silence (new in 1.1.0)

The tempo climbs on its own (+5 BPM every 8 bars…) up to your target, and muted bars to test your inner sense of time.

## Controls

- **Space**: start or stop.
- **Left and right arrows**: tempo ±1 · **up and down**: ±5.
- **T**: tap tempo · **A**: accent on the first beat.
- Click a **beat** to change its accent: strong, normal or muted.

## Features

- **30 to 260** BPM, signatures from **2/4 to 12/8**, eighths, triplets and sixteenths, four sounds.
- Clicks are scheduled on the audio clock and stay in time even when the tab is in the background.

- **Practice**: a program raises the tempo on its own (+2 BPM every 4 bars, +5 every 8…) up to your target tempo, shown in blue while it climbs. **Silence** mutes some bars (1 in 2, 2 in 4…) to check you keep time without the click.

## Data and sync

Your settings are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on.
